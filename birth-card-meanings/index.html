Birth Card Meanings: All 52 Cards & the Joker

Your birth card is the card your birthday maps to, and its meaning describes your core personality, natural strengths, and life lesson. In Cardy it's called your Inner Child card. The meaning comes from its suit (the area of life it rules) and its rank (its stage of growth). Find your card below.

Birth card meanings by suit

♥ Hearts — the lovers

Warm, relational, and emotionally attuned, Hearts govern romance, family, artistic expression, and heartfelt connection.

Ace of Hearts — The Desire for Love & Awakening

The pioneer of the heart. You possess a restless craving for affection, creative expression, and new emotional horizons. Passionate, magnetic, and eager to begin new chapters, you love with youthful enthusiasm. Your primary life lesson is cultivating inner self-love and emotional self-reliance so you do not chase validation from others at the expense of your own peace.

2 of Hearts — The Lover's Card & Sacred Union

Deeply devoted, affectionate, and wired for intimate partnership. You flourish when sharing your life, projects, and secrets with a kindred spirit. You are an exceptional companion with natural romantic charm, but your karmic journey requires overcoming codependency, people-pleasing, and the fear of solitude so your unions remain equal and empowering.

3 of Hearts — The Creative Explorer & Variety

An expressive artist and social butterfly gifted with immense charm, playful wit, and emotional curiosity. You are drawn to multiple passions and social circles simultaneously. While your versatility makes you fascinating, your central challenge is romantic and creative indecision—learning that depth of commitment yields far richer joy than endless novelty.

4 of Hearts — The Marriage Card & Emotional Security

Stable, loyal, and deeply protective of family and lifelong companions. You are the emotional foundation of your community, building safe, dependable spaces where loved ones thrive. Because stability matters so much to you, your growth lies in releasing emotional stubbornness and trusting life when relationships require transformation.

5 of Hearts — The Restless Heart & Emotional Adventurer

Curious, freedom-loving, and driven by an appetite for emotional adventure and travel. You resist feeling fenced in by routine or rigid expectations and enjoy meeting diverse personalities. Your fulfillment comes when you learn to cultivate inner freedom, allowing you to sustain deep emotional roots without feeling an urge to flee when things get vulnerable.

6 of Hearts — The Peacemaker & Karmic Love

A natural healer and diplomat centered on peace, fairness, and domestic responsibility. You carry a strong sense of karmic justice in relationships, often acting as the mediator who restores harmony. Your vital life lesson is learning healthy boundaries: forgiving without enabling, and knowing when an old emotional debt has already been paid.

7 of Hearts — The Spiritual Lover & Unconditional Heart

A card of high emotional sensitivity, deep intuition, and spiritual awakening through love. You hold romantic ideals to an elevated standard, which can occasionally lead to heartache if you expect perfection from mortals. When you elevate your love beyond possessiveness into unconditional compassion, your intuitive and healing gifts shine brilliantly.

8 of Hearts — The Card of Emotional Magnetism & Charm

Magnetic, captivating, and socially formidable. You possess a natural gift for charming crowds, winning allies, and commanding affection without force. People naturally gravitate to your warmth. Your responsibility is to wield this emotional authority with genuine integrity, resisting the temptation to manipulate hearts to preserve control.

9 of Hearts — The Universal Giver & Sacred Completion

Compassionate, generous, and attuned to the broader human condition. You are here to graduate from personal attachments into universal love, often championing causes and helping others through emotional closure. By embracing release with an open heart rather than resisting endings, you step into profound wisdom and spiritual fulfillment.

10 of Hearts — The Radiant Community & Social Success

Charismatic, uplifting, and born to shine in front of groups. You possess a celebratory spirit and love bringing people together in joyous, artistic, or social gatherings. While you effortlessly gain public admiration, your challenge is maintaining genuine, grounded emotional intimacy in private life away from the applause.

Jack of Hearts — The Devoted Sacrifice & Youthful Romantic

Playful, idealistic, and driven by selfless devotion to the people and causes you love. You carry the eternal spirit of youth, poetry, and romance, frequently stepping up as a protector or savior. Your path to maturity involves grounding your ideals in reality so you do not sacrifice your well-being for unreciprocated dreams.

Queen of Hearts — The Loving Mother & Empathetic Nurturer

Warm, regal, highly intuitive, and instinctively supportive. You radiate unconditional empathy, domestic beauty, and emotional wisdom, creating environments where everyone feels seen and cared for. Your lifelong lesson is to turn that same boundless care inward, honoring your own needs with the same reverence you show to others.

King of Hearts — The Master of the Heart & Emotional Patriarch

Wise, protective, composed, and fatherly. You command natural authority in personal and professional relationships, maintaining emotional mastery and fair leadership even amidst crisis. Your greatest strength is leading with a compassionate heart, resisting emotional detachment when difficult decisions must be made.

♣ Clubs — the thinkers

Curious, verbal, and idea-driven, Clubs govern the intellect, communication, curiosity, and the pursuit of knowledge.

Ace of Clubs — The Seeker of Truth & Spark of Curiosity

Fueled by an unquenchable thirst for knowledge, books, and fresh ideas. You possess an active, pioneering intellect that is forever asking questions and uncovering hidden perspectives. Your challenge is overcoming mental anxiety and over-analysis by pairing your brilliant mental spark with intuitive stillness and decisive action.

2 of Clubs — The Inquisitive Conversationalist & Mental Match

Gifted in dialogue, debate, and intellectual companionship. You think best out loud and flourish when collaborating with a partner who sharpens your intellect. While highly communicative and socially engaging, your growth comes from quieting mental restlessness and learning to trust your own independent judgment.

3 of Clubs — The Creative Genius & Mental Dynamo

A rapid-fire writer, speaker, or innovator bursting with creative concepts. Highly versatile and witty, you can communicate across multiple mediums and captivate audiences with ease. Your central challenge is focus: resisting the urge to juggle too many ideas at once so your most brilliant visions reach completion.

4 of Clubs — The Structured Mind & Stable Knowledge

Grounded, practical, organized, and mentally resilient. You excel at building frameworks, systems, methodologies, and clear educational paths that stand the test of time. Your lesson is remaining intellectually flexible, welcoming new viewpoints and modern evidence rather than clinging dogmatically to fixed beliefs.

5 of Clubs — The Inquisitive Traveler & Mental Wanderer

Hungry for adventure, philosophical shifts, and diverse viewpoints. You despise intellectual monotony and routine, preferring to explore fresh philosophies, cultures, and ways of living. When you channel your restless curiosity into a specialized field of study or purposeful travel, your insights inspire everyone around you.

6 of Clubs — The Messenger of Truth & Intuitive Clarity

Calm, reliable, and deeply committed to intellectual integrity and clear communication. You carry a natural talent for recognizing universal truths and acting as a trustworthy counselor. Your test is standing firm in your convictions without yielding to peer pressure or compromising what you know is true simply to avoid debate.

7 of Clubs — The Spiritual Intellect & Overcoming Worry

Intuitive, visionary, and philosophically inclined. You possess profound mental perception that pierces surface appearances, yet you may struggle with overthinking and chronic worry. When you train your mind to focus on spiritual faith and positive visualization rather than worst-case scenarios, your mind becomes an instrument of remarkable inspiration.

8 of Clubs — The Mental Powerhouse & Intellectual Authority

Mentally formidable, persuasive, and exceptionally disciplined. You possess intense focus and executive intelligence, capable of achieving remarkable intellectual and organizational milestones through steady concentration. Your challenge is avoiding intellectual arrogance and listening open-heartedly to differing perspectives.

9 of Clubs — The Universal Thinker & Philosophical Completion

Broad-minded, philanthropic, and naturally wise. You synthesize complex worldviews and mentor others, using your voice to educate and uplift on a large scale. Your journey involves releasing obsolete philosophies and outgrown opinions, continually making room for higher spiritual understanding.

10 of Clubs — The Radiant Teacher & Public Brilliance

Exceptionally articulate, widely read, and born to communicate to the public. You succeed as a teacher, author, media creator, or public strategist, translating difficult concepts into accessible, engaging wisdom. Your test is balancing your intense mental output with physical exercise and restorative silence.

Jack of Clubs — The Clever Strategist & Creative Trickster

Ingenious, witty, charismatic, and always ten steps ahead. You thrive in technology, marketing, law, and creative problem-solving, reading situations instantly. Your destiny asks you to anchor your razor-sharp cleverness in unwavering ethics and truth, using your inventive mind to build rather than outmaneuver.

Queen of Clubs — The Intuitive Counselor & Practical Queen

Perceptive, hardworking, and gifted with razor-sharp business instincts and intuitive foresight. You lead through organized wisdom, executive capability, and psychic clarity. Your growth lies in delegating responsibilities and guarding against mental exhaustion from carrying everyone's problems on your own shoulders.

King of Clubs — The Master of the Mind & Executive Intellect

Commanding, strategic, authoritative, and visionary. You are an intellectual heavyweight and natural industry leader whose mental clarity allows you to govern enterprises and philosophies with quiet confidence. You excel at the helm, called to govern with justice and open-minded wisdom.

♦ Diamonds — the builders

Value-driven, ambitious, and resourceful, Diamonds govern personal self-worth, financial acumen, commerce, and material mastery.

Ace of Diamonds — The Ambition for Worth & Financial Drive

Enterprising, self-reliant, and motivated by tangible achievement. You possess a keen eye for commerce, business creation, and investment opportunities. Your path teaches you that authentic wealth begins within: as you cultivate unshakeable inner self-esteem, outer prosperity and material resources flow naturally.

2 of Diamonds — The Business Partner & Dealmaker

Diplomatic, commercially savvy, and skilled in collaborative enterprise. You generate wealth through joint ventures, contracts, and mutual investments. While brilliant at financial reciprocity, your lesson is separating financial value from human connection, making sure love is never treated as a transactional bargain.

3 of Diamonds — The Financial Innovator & Multiple Interests

Versatile, entrepreneurial, and creative in commerce. You often juggle multiple income streams, investments, and artistic ventures simultaneously. You thrive when you overcome financial anxiety and indecision by focusing your energy on one or two high-value ventures that truly align with your values.

4 of Diamonds — The Bedrock of Wealth & Material Security

Methodical, hardworking, and dedicated to building enduring financial stability. You are the rock of family and business assets, building lasting wealth step by step. Because security is your priority, your growth lies in releasing fear of scarcity and enjoying the fruits of your labor with generosity and ease.

5 of Diamonds — The Commercial Adventurer & Changing Fortunes

Free-spirited, adaptive, and drawn to diverse ventures, trading, and travel. You understand market cycles intuitively and navigate change with agility. Your lesson is cultivating internal stability and financial grounding so that shifts in external circumstances do not destabilize your sense of self-worth.

6 of Diamonds — The Settler of Debts & Financial Balancer

Fair, generous, and committed to financial integrity and karmic settling. You have a steady money karma and an instinct for equitable compensation and philanthropic giving. Your challenge is learning to charge what you are truly worth without feeling guilty for claiming your rightful abundance.

7 of Diamonds — The Spiritual Millionaire & True Value

Intuitive, discerning, and spiritually tested around money and material assets. You discover that when you fixate on material scarcity you struggle, but when you align your work with spiritual purpose and trust divine timing, abundance flows effortlessly into your life.

8 of Diamonds — The Sun Card of Wealth & Executive Power

Imposing, prosperous, and naturally influential in business. You wield executive clout and financial magnetism with ease, born to manage large capital, organizations, or prominent enterprises. Your higher call is using your financial power to empower others and elevate your community.

9 of Diamonds — The Philanthropist & Release of Material Ego

Generous, worldly, and driven by higher humanitarian values over mere accumulation. You learn the ultimate lesson of the Diamonds suit: that true richness lies in giving back, letting go of material control, and sharing your abundance freely with the world.

10 of Diamonds — The Pinnacle of Success & Commercial Titan

Highly ambitious, capable, and born for grand-scale prosperity. You possess the drive to build prominent businesses, accumulate substantial resources, and achieve public renown. Your challenge is grounding your identity in character and spiritual truth rather than the size of your balance sheet.

Jack of Diamonds — The Enterprising Trader & Charming Promoter

Shrewd, persuasive, and magnetically creative in sales, technology, and enterprise. You are an energetic dealmaker who spots opportunities before anyone else. Your destiny invites you to hold the highest ethical standards, ensuring your commercial genius serves long-term trust and mutual prosperity.

Queen of Diamonds — The Gracious Patron & Business Maven

Elegant, astute, and gifted with a refined appreciation for quality, luxury, and fine craftsmanship. You know how to cultivate, manage, and protect assets with graceful efficiency. Your lifelong lesson is balancing your generosity toward others with sensible boundaries and authentic self-care.

King of Diamonds — The Master of Commerce & Industry Titan

Decisive, authoritative, and seasoned in the laws of value, enterprise, and capital. You sit comfortably atop financial and organizational hierarchies, leading with pragmatic strength and executive vision. You command respect naturally, guided to lead with honor and fair distribution of rewards.

♠ Spades — the sages

Deep, disciplined, and transformative, Spades govern physical labor, health, career mastery, endurance, and spiritual awakening.

Ace of Spades — The Secret of Transformation & Inner Will

The ancient card of profound metamorphosis, ambition, and spiritual awakening. You possess immense resolve to overcome hardship and reinvent yourself across a lifetime. Your journey asks you to surrender the ego's need for control, discovering that true power emerges when you align your labor with spiritual devotion.

2 of Spades — The Dedicated Partner & Fellowship of Labor

Reliable, loyal, and committed to common causes in work, health, and craftsmanship. You excel in long-term professional partnerships and shared physical or spiritual missions. Your challenge is maintaining your personal autonomy, ensuring you don't shoulder an unfair portion of the workload.

3 of Spades — The Master Craftsman & Creative Effort

Energetic, multitalented, and capable of turning physical effort into fine art. You excel across varied trades, design disciplines, or holistic healing arts. Your challenge is learning work-life balance—avoiding chronic overwork and scattered energy by grounding yourself in one craft at a time.

4 of Spades — The Unshakable Foundation & Pillar of Health

Resilient, grounded, and possessing an iron constitution and work ethic. You build enduring structures and take great pride in dependable, honest labor. Your lesson is remaining open to innovation and flexibility when life asks you to change established routines or methods.

5 of Spades — The Restless Wanderer & Life Reinventor

Dynamic, bold, and unafraid of major career transitions, physical relocations, or radical lifestyle overhauls. You despise feeling confined and embrace life’s turning points. When you anchor your transformations to a meaningful spiritual or professional mission, your adaptability becomes your greatest superpower.

6 of Spades — The Card of Fate & The Steadfast Laborer

Steadfast, karmically disciplined, and aligned with personal destiny. You understand that actions carry consequences, navigating work and health with unwavering integrity and calm endurance. You rarely waver under pressure, challenged only to avoid fatalism and embrace your ability to shape outcomes.

7 of Spades — The Spiritual Healer & Faith Under Pressure

Deep, mystical, and resilient. You often encounter pivotal life lessons around physical health, endurance, or demanding workloads, discovering that inner faith, mindfulness, and surrender dissolve outer obstacles. You have profound gifts for natural healing and spiritual teaching.

8 of Spades — The Power of Labor & Unstoppable Willpower

Formidable, healing, and possessing immense stamina and executive drive. You overcome enormous obstacles through sheer perseverance and focused physical energy. Your challenge is learning to temper your iron will with gentleness, listening to your body’s need for rest and showing patience toward others.

9 of Spades — The Universal Sage & The Great Completion

Philosophically profound, compassionate, and spiritually mature. You assist others through life’s major endings, grief, and transformations, carrying the deck's oldest wisdom. By releasing personal burdens and embracing spiritual service, you find supreme peace and universal purpose.

10 of Spades — The Titan of Achievement & Public Mastery

The card of towering career success, supreme effort, and monumental accomplishment. You have the stamina to carry entire enterprises, projects, or causes on your shoulders. Your growth lies in recognizing when to step back, delegate, and replenish your spiritual well-being away from worldly duty.

Jack of Spades — The Visionary Initiate & Spiritual Actor

Clever, mystical, charismatic, and multifaceted. You operate with ease between spiritual awareness and practical mastery, blessed with creative vision and dramatic flair. Your destiny asks you to hold the highest ethical standards on your path, channeling your gifts into transformative, uplifting work.

Queen of Spades — The Sovereign of Self-Mastery & Practical Wisdom

Disciplined, capable, intuitive, and deeply authoritative. You combine spiritual insight with organizational prowess, inspiring others through selfless service and quiet dignity. You are born to lead by example, called to temper your exacting standards with warm compassion for human imperfection.

King of Spades — The Crown of Wisdom & Supreme Master

The highest card in the entire deck. Regal, principled, and seasoned by life’s ultimate lessons, you hold innate authority across work, philosophy, and executive leadership. You govern with impartial grace and calm majesty, charged to use your supreme authority for the highest good of all.

🃏 The Joker

The cosmic wildcard of the deck, ruling December 31 birthdays.

The Joker — The Cosmic Wildcard

Bound by no single suit or fixed trajectory. Born on December 31, you embody pure adaptability, theatricality, independence, and wit. You are capable of mirroring any card in the deck and writing your own rules as you go, finding your highest expression when you use your freedom to illuminate the game of life with joy and wisdom.

Beyond the birth card

Your birth card is one of three. Cardology also calculates your Chosen Purpose card, using your zodiac sign, and your Highest Self card, which combines the two. You also have two karma cards. (What Are Karma Cards? →)

Birth card meanings FAQ

Can two people have the same birth card?

Yes. Everyone born on the same date shares one. Your other two cards add the nuance.

Is my birth card my zodiac sign?

No, but they're related: cardology uses your sign to calculate your second card. (Cardology vs Astrology →)

What if I was born on December 31?

You're the Joker, the deck's wildcard.
