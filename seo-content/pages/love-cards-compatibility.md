# Love Cards Compatibility: Reading Your Relationship

**Love cards compatibility reads a romantic relationship through the 52-card destiny system.** Popularized by Robert Lee Camp's book *Love Cards*, it looks at the link between two people's cards to explain why you were drawn together, what each of you gives, and where you'll be tested.

## What goes into a love reading

- **Suits:** how each of you loves
- **Ranks:** the balance of maturity between you
- **Planetary links:** the type of connection, like Venus or Saturn
- **Karma cards:** whether one of you is the other's gift or lesson

## How each suit loves

| Suit | Loves through | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| **♥ Hearts** | Feeling and tenderness | Warmth and appreciation |
| **♣ Clubs** | Conversation and curiosity | A partner who keeps them thinking |
| **♦ Diamonds** | Building a shared future | Respect and shared values |
| **♠ Spades** | Loyalty through hard times | A partner to take on life with |

## Four common dynamics

- **Venus and Mars:** Mars brings the spark; Venus keeps it warm.
- **Karma cards:** if one partner's birth card is the other's karma card, the pull is intense. One of you is there to give, the other to learn. *([What Are Karma Cards? →])*
- **Saturn:** one partner steadies and teaches the other. It can feel heavy, but it's common in long marriages.
- **Moon:** being together feels like coming home.

## Beyond one card

Classic love cards readings compare one card per person. Cardy compares all three, so you see how you love by instinct, what you look for in a partner, and what you become together.

## Love cards compatibility FAQ

**What if our birth cards have no direct link?**
That's common. Cardy also checks your second and third cards and both spreads, where links often show up.

**Can love cards say whether we'll last?**
They show how you trigger and support each other. How long you last depends on what you do with that.

**Are opposite suits a bad sign?**
No. Different suits often balance each other well.
