# What Are Karma Cards? Your Two Hidden Cards

**Karma cards are two cards linked to your birth card: one that supports you and one that challenges you.** The supporting card, which Cardy calls your Blessing card, is a gift that comes easily. The challenging card, your Duty card, is a lesson that keeps coming back until you learn it. Together they explain why some things feel effortless and others feel like homework.

## How they're found

Your karma cards are fixed by your birth card's place in the deck. Everyone who shares your birth card shares your karma cards. Nothing is drawn or guessed.

## What they tell you

- **Your supporting card** shows where you have grace to lean on.
- **Your challenging card** shows the pattern you're here to work through.
- **Read together**, they explain your natural push and pull.

## Karma cards FAQ

**How many karma cards do I have?**
Two: one supporting and one challenging.

**Are karma cards the same as my three main cards?**
No. Your main cards are Inner Child, Chosen Purpose, and Highest Self. Karma cards are a separate layer. *([What Is Cardology? →])*

**Can my karma cards change?**
No. They're fixed by your birth card. What changes is how you work with them.
