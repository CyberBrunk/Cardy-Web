# What Are Karma Cards? Your Two Hidden Cards Explained

**Your karma cards are two specific cards linked to your birth card — one that supports you and one that challenges you.** In cardology, they represent a karmic exchange: the **supporting karma card** (Cardy calls it your *Blessing Card*) is a gift you were dealt, a strength that comes easily; the **challenging karma card** (your *Duty Card*) is a lesson you're here to work through. Together they explain why certain things feel effortless and others feel like your life's assignment. Here's what karma cards are, how they're determined, and what yours reveal.

## What are karma cards?

Karma cards are a pair of cards permanently connected to your birth card through the structure of the deck. You don't draw them — like everything in cardology, they're fixed by your birth card:

- **Supporting Karma Card (Blessing / Karma +)** — a natural gift, an area of grace
- **Challenging Karma Card (Duty / Karma −)** — a recurring lesson, an area of growth

## The two karma cards

### Your Supporting Karma Card

This is the card that has your back — talents and support that show up almost without effort. It's karmic "credit" you carry into this life. *([Supporting Karma Card →])*

### Your Challenging Karma Card

This is the card that asks something of you — the pattern you're here to master. Worked consciously, it becomes one of your greatest strengths. *([Challenging Karma Card →])*

## How karma cards are determined

Your karma cards are set by your birth card's position in the deck's underlying structure — a fixed relationship, the same for everyone who shares your birth card. There's nothing to guess; the system assigns them.

## What your karma cards reveal about you

- **Where you're gifted** — the supporting card shows grace you can lean on
- **Where you're stretched** — the challenging card shows the lesson that keeps returning
- **How they balance** — read together, they explain your natural push-and-pull

## Karma cards FAQ

**How many karma cards do I have?**
Two — one supporting (Blessing), one challenging (Duty).

**Are karma cards the same as my three main cards?**
No. Your three main cards are Inner Child, Chosen Purpose, and Highest Self. Karma cards are a separate, deeper layer. *([What Is Cardology? →])*

**Can I change my karma cards?**
No — they're fixed by your birth card. What changes is how consciously you work with them.
