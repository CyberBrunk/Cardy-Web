# Your Supporting Karma Card: The Gift You Were Dealt

**Your supporting karma card, called the Blessing card in Cardy, is a strength that comes to you easily.** It's the talent other people notice before you do, the area where things tend to flow. Where your challenging card asks for effort, this one offers grace. Knowing it lets you use it on purpose instead of taking it for granted.

## What it is

It's one of your two karma cards, and it's fixed by your birth card. *([What Are Karma Cards? →])*

## Where the gift lives

- **♥ Hearts:** warmth, love, connection
- **♣ Clubs:** ideas, words, learning
- **♦ Diamonds:** value, resources, self-worth
- **♠ Spades:** steady, deep, transformative work

## How to use it

- **Name it.** Gifts are easy to undervalue because they feel easy.
- **Use it on purpose.** Put it where it does the most good.
- **Let it balance your Duty card.** Your gift is often what helps you meet your lesson.

## Supporting karma card FAQ

**Do I have to earn it?**
No. It's a gift. The work is remembering to use it.

**Does everyone have both kinds of karma card?**
Yes. Everyone has one of each. *([Challenging Karma Card →])*

**Is it part of my birth card?**
No. It's a separate card that reinforces your natural strengths. *([Birth Card Meanings →])*
