# Your Supporting Karma Card: The Gift You Were Dealt

**Your supporting karma card — called the Blessing Card in cardology — is a natural gift woven into your chart: a strength that comes easily, almost like karmic credit you were born with.** Where your challenging karma card asks for effort, your supporting card offers grace. ## What is a supporting karma card?

It's one of your two karma cards — the one that **supports** you rather than tests you. Cardy calls it your **Blessing Card (Karma +)**. Like all karma cards, it's fixed by your birth card, shared by everyone who holds the same card.
