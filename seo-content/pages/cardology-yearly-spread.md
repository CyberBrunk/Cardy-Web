## The seven 52-day periods

| Period | Days after your birthday | Planet | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | 1–52 | Mercury | Ideas, conversations, quick moves |
| **2** | 53–104 | Venus | Love, friends, beauty, home |
| **3** | 105–156 | Mars | Drive, energy, some friction |
| **4** | 157–208 | Jupiter | Growth, money, good luck |
| **5** | 209–260 | Saturn | Discipline, health, responsibility |
| **6** | 261–312 | Uranus | Surprises and breakthroughs |
| **7** | 313–364 | Neptune | Reflection, dreams, travel |

## Key cards in a yearly spread

- **Long Range card:** the theme of the whole year
- **Pluto card:** the year's biggest challenge
- **Result card:** what you gain by working through it
- **Environment card:** the support around you
- **Displacement card:** where your energy gets pulled this year

## How it changes

Your spread starts on your birthday, not January 1. Each birthday your cards move one position, so every year brings a new set.

## Cardology yearly spread FAQ

**When does my yearly spread start?**
On your birthday. It runs until the day before your next one.

**Does it change every year?**
Yes. Each birthday brings a new spread.

**Can it predict events?**
It shows timing and themes, like when love or growth is favored, rather than specific events.
