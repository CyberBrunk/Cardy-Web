## What is a cardology yearly spread?

In cardology, every year of your life (from birthday to birthday) corresponds to a unique shift across the **Grand Solar Spread**. As you age, your Birth Card steps into a new position, drawing down a fresh hand of cards to govern that 365-day cycle.

Your yearly spread reveals:

- The overall **theme** of your year (what you are meant to build or release).
- Your **52-day timeline** (when to push forward, when to pause, when love blooms, and when career tests arrive).
- The **karmic lesson** of the year (your Pluto and Saturn cards).
- The ultimate **payoff** of your efforts (your Result card).

## The seven 52-day planetary periods

Cardology divides your 365-day year into seven equal cycles of **52 days each** ($7 \times 52 = 364 \text{ days}$, plus day 365 as your birthday transition). Each period is ruled by a planet that dictates the tempo and focus:

| Period | Days from Birthday | Ruling Planet | Primary Life Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Days 1–52 | **Mercury ☿** | Mental clarity, ideas, communication, short trips, quick deals |
| **2** | Days 53–104 | **Venus ♀** | Romance, social life, art, beauty, affection, domestic harmony |
| **3** | Days 105–156 | **Mars ♂** | Drive, physical vitality, assertiveness, ambition, potential friction |
| **4** | Days 157–208 | **Jupiter ♃** | Expansion, financial blessings, luck, business growth, generosity |
| **5** | Days 209–260 | **Saturn ♄** | Discipline, health checks, heavy responsibilities, karmic reality |
| **6** | Days 261–312 | **Uranus ♅** | Sudden changes, unexpected breakthroughs, innovation, freedom |
| **7** | Days 313–364 | **Neptune ♆** | Spiritual reflection, dreams, travel, surrender, completing cycles |

## Key anchor cards in your yearly spread

When looking at a full yearly spread, five key positions anchor your annual story:

1. **The Long Range Card:** The overarching background music of your entire year. Whatever suit and rank lands here sets the primary lesson you will grapple with.
2. **The Pluto Card:** The most challenging card of your year. It represents deep subconscious transformation — what you must let die in order to grow.
3. **The Result Card:** The gift waiting on the other side of your Pluto challenge. This card shows the harvest of your year's work.
4. **The Environment Card:** The people and external circumstances surrounding you. A favorable card here means ease and support from others.
5. **The Displacement Card:** The card whose natural place your Birth Card occupies this year. It reveals where your energy is being channeled.

## Cardology yearly spread FAQ

**When does my cardology yearly spread begin and end?**
Your yearly spread begins on the exact date of your birthday and runs until the day before your next birthday. It does not follow the calendar year from January 1.

**Does my yearly spread change every year?**
Yes. Every year on your birthday, your cards advance one position in the age sequence, giving you a completely new set of cards and planetary influences for that age.

**Can a yearly spread predict specific events?**
Cardology reveals archetypal patterns, timing windows, and psychological dynamics. It shows *when* certain energy (like romance in Venus or expansion in Jupiter) is heightened, empowering you to make the most aligned choices.
