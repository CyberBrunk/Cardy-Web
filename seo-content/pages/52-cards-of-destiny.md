# The 52 Cards of Destiny: How the Deck Maps Your Life

**The 52 cards of destiny are the ordinary playing-card deck, read as a calendar of human life.** Each card lines up with a week of the year and with specific birthdays, and each has its own personality. Your birthday points to one of them: your Birth Card.

## Why 52 cards?

| The deck | The calendar |
|---|---|
| 52 cards | 52 weeks |
| 4 suits | 4 seasons |
| 13 cards per suit | 13 weeks per season, 13 lunar cycles a year |
| All card values added up (364), plus the Joker | 365 days |

## The four suits

- **♥ Hearts:** love, family, feelings
- **♣ Clubs:** ideas, words, learning
- **♦ Diamonds:** money, values, self-worth
- **♠ Spades:** work, health, wisdom, change

## The ranks

- **Ace to 10:** stages of growth, from a new start (Ace) to fullness (10)
- **Jack, Queen, King:** maturity. The Jack is young and clever, the Queen nurtures, the King leads.
- **Joker:** the wildcard, for December 31 birthdays

A card's meaning is its suit plus its rank. The 4♥ is about building a safe, loving home; the King♠ is about mastery earned through hard work. *([Destiny Cards Meanings →])*

## The 52 cards of destiny FAQ

**Is it a special deck?**
No. It's the standard 52-card deck, read as a system instead of a game.

**Where does the Joker fit?**
It's the 53rd card, for people born on December 31.

**Is this the same as the "Cards of Your Destiny" book?**
It's the same tradition, popularized by Robert Lee Camp. *([Robert Lee Camp & Destiny Cards →])*
