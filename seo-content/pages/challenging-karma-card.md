# Your Challenging Karma Card: Working Through the Lesson

**Your challenging karma card, called the Duty card in Cardy, is the lesson your life keeps handing you until you learn it.** It's the pattern that repeats: the reaction you fall back on under stress, or the theme that shows up in every chapter. It isn't a curse. Worked with on purpose, it often becomes one of your biggest strengths.

## What it is

It's one of your two karma cards, and it's fixed by your birth card. *([What Are Karma Cards? →])*

## Where the lesson lives

The card's suit tells you:

- **♥ Hearts:** love, boundaries, honesty about feelings
- **♣ Clubs:** communication, truth, overthinking
- **♦ Diamonds:** money, worth, what you value
- **♠ Spades:** work, health, letting go

## How to work with it

- **Notice the pattern.** Where does it keep showing up?
- **Pick the harder, wiser move.** Respond instead of reacting.
- **Give it time.** Progress is measured in months, not days. Journaling helps you spot it.

## Challenging karma card FAQ

**Is a challenging karma card bad?**
No. It's a growth edge, not a flaw.

**Is it the same as my birth card?**
No. It's a separate card linked to your birth card.

**What balances it?**
Your supporting karma card, the gift that helps you meet the lesson. *([Supporting Karma Card →])*
